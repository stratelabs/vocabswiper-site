Vocabulary flashcards you swipe

Vocab Swiper is a flashcard app built for learning a language's vocabulary. Every card is a real word with audio and an example sentence. Recall it, reveal it, swipe to grade yourself, and spaced repetition decides when you see it again.

How the flashcards work

1Recall firstYou see the word and try to remember what it means before you reveal it. Peeking counts as a miss.
2Swipe to gradeRight if you knew it, left if you didn't. One motion, about two seconds a card.
3Spaced repetitionFSRS brings each card back right before you'd forget it, so you review less and remember more.
4Then use itAs words stick, you spell them, type them, say them out loud and read them in short stories.

Questions

Is Vocab Swiper free?

Yes. It's free to download on iPhone and Android, and the flashcards are free to learn with. An optional Pro subscription adds premium voices and some advanced features.

What is spaced repetition?

A way of timing reviews. Each card comes back just before you'd forget it: soon after you learn it, then at longer and longer gaps as it sticks. It takes far fewer reviews than going through a pile of flashcards in order. Vocab Swiper uses FSRS, a modern spaced repetition algorithm.

Why swipe instead of flipping a card?

Grading yourself is one motion, so a review takes a couple of seconds and you can do ten in the queue for coffee. Swipe right if you knew it, left if you didn't. Peeking at the answer first counts as a miss.

Can I add my own words?

Yes. Met a word in a lesson, a show or a menu? Tap + and add it. It goes into your own deck, next to the ready-made ones.

Which languages are there?

56 languages, each with a real graded deck of thousands of words: Japanese (JLPT N5 to N1), Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and many more. See them all on the languages page.
