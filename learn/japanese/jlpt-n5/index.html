What JLPT N5 covers

N5 is the first JLPT level: reading simple phrases written in hiragana, katakana and basic kanji, and following slow, everyday conversation about familiar things. This list has 629 words. It is the first level, so this is where everyone starts.

Questions about JLPT N5 vocabulary

How many words do you need for JLPT N5?

There is no official number. This list has 629 words at JLPT N5, and 629 counting every level up to it. Study lists differ in size because each one is compiled independently.

Is this the official JLPT N5 list?

No. The JLPT has not published vocabulary lists since 2010. This list builds on the most widely used community lists, with every reading checked against a dictionary and duplicate spellings removed.

What is the fastest way to learn these words?

Test yourself rather than re-reading. Trying to recall a word before you see its meaning, then reviewing it again just before you would forget it (spaced repetition), takes far fewer reviews than reading a list over and over.
