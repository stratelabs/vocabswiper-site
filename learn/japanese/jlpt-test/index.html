What's your JLPT level?

A free vocabulary test from N5 to N1. 30 quick questions, about three minutes, no sign-up.

How it works

  • You see a Japanese word and pick its meaning from four.
  • It starts easy and gets harder, 6 words per level.
  • Don't guess: I don't know is the honest answer, and wrong guesses count against you.

Your result

This measures vocabulary only. The real JLPT also tests grammar, reading and listening, so treat it as a guide to which words to study next.

Close the gap to your next level.

Vocab Swiper's Japanese deck has every JLPT word from N5 to N1. Recall each one, hear it, and let spaced repetition bring it back right before you'd forget it.
