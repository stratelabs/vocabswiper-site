Questions

What Japanese phrases should I learn before a trip?

Start with すみません (sumimasen: excuse me, sorry, and thank you), これください (kore kudasai: I'll take this), and 大丈夫です (daijōbu desu: no thanks, I'm fine). With those three and pointing, you can get through most shops and restaurants.

Do I need to speak Japanese to travel in Japan?

No. Signs in cities and on trains are in English too, and staff are used to visitors. A few phrases make every interaction smoother, and people really do appreciate the effort.

How do I pronounce Japanese?

Japanese sounds are simpler than they look. Vowels are always a (ah), i (ee), u (oo), e (eh), o (oh), and a line over a vowel (ō, ū) just means hold it twice as long. Tap the speaker next to any phrase to hear it.
