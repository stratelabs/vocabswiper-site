German false friends

German and English grew from the same roots, so plenty of words still match: Hand, Haus, Finger. But some drifted. These 21 look like English and mean something else, and two of them come up in almost every first conversation.

Questions about German false friends

What is a false friend?

A false friend is a word that looks or sounds like a word in another language but means something different. Teachers also call them false cognates. The danger is that they feel safe: you don't think to check a word you already “know”.

Which German false friend causes the most trouble?

Bekommen. It means to get, not to become, so “Ich bekomme ein Steak” in a restaurant is perfectly normal. Gift (poison) is the funniest one, but bekommen is the one you'll actually trip over.

How do you avoid false friends?

Learn words in sentences instead of guessing from how they look. When a new word looks like English, check it once. Most of the time it really is the same, which is exactly why the exceptions catch people out.
