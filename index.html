Tappable captions

Learn the words in the video, while you watch it

Interactive subtitles follow along word by word. See one you don't know? Tap it.

  • Tap for the meaning. The video pauses, the word's meaning comes up, and a tap anywhere carries on.
  • Save it in one tap. Add the word to your deck and it joins your flashcard reviews.
  • Works for Japanese, Chinese and Korean. Sentences are split into real words, even where the language has no spaces.

Vocab swiping

For when you actually want to study

No brainrot required. Vocab Swiper is also a full vocabulary flashcard app with spaced repetition: thousands of ready-made words per language, levelled from beginner to advanced.

  • Swipe to grade yourself. Right if you knew it, left if you didn't. Ten reviews fit in a coffee queue.
  • Honest recall. The meaning stays hidden until you've tried. Peeking counts as a miss.
  • Spaced repetition that works. FSRS schedules each word for just before you'd forget it.

Questions

Can you really learn a language by watching short videos?
Videos are the best listening practice there is: real people, real speed, the words people actually use. On their own they wash over you. Vocab Swiper puts captions on each video that you can tap word by word, and any word you save goes into flashcards that come back until you know it.
How do the tappable captions work?
Captions run along the bottom of each video, with each word lighting up as it's said and a translation underneath. Tap a word and the video pauses to show what it means. One more tap adds it to your deck.
Which languages can I learn?
Flashcard decks cover fifty-six languages, including Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic and Hindi. The video feed covers more than thirty of them. See every language and its words →
Is Vocab Swiper free?
Yes. It's free to download on iPhone and Android, and every deck, spaced repetition, streaks and quizzes are free. Free accounts swipe 50 flashcards a day. An optional Pro subscription adds unlimited swiping, natural-sounding voices, unlimited AI example sentences and reading, and custom topic decks.
How is it different from other language learning apps?
Most apps teach you sentences written for a lesson. Vocab Swiper starts from real videos made by native speakers, then uses flashcards with honest recall and FSRS spaced repetition so the words stay. There are no leagues and no guilt trips. Coming from a flashcard app? See how it compares with Quizlet →
Does it work offline?
The flashcard decks and your reviews live on your phone, so studying works on a plane. Videos, AI example sentences and premium voices need a connection.
