Q&A

Which languages can I learn?
Fifty-six and counting: Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Icelandic, Finnish, Greek, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Romanian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Serbian, Slovenian, Lithuanian, Latvian, Estonian, Albanian, Georgian, Armenian, Irish, Welsh, Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Hindi, Bengali, Urdu, Punjabi, Tamil, Marathi, Gujarati, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Malay, Filipino, Samoan, Swahili, Zulu, Yoruba and Amharic. Each with a real graded deck of thousands of words, not a demo list, plus English itself. See every language and its words →
What's free, and what's Pro?
Free: every deck, spaced repetition, streaks, quizzes, and an example sentence for each word. Pro: natural human-sounding voices, unlimited AI example sentences and in-context reading, custom topic decks, and a streak shield for the day life gets in the way.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The decks and your reviews live on your phone, so learning works on a plane. AI-generated example sentences and premium voices need a connection; everything else doesn't.
How is this different from other flashcard apps?
Honest recall, and it doesn't stop at cards. The meaning stays hidden until you've tried; revealing counts as a miss, so your stats can't lie to you. As a word sticks, the app makes you produce it: spell it from tiles, type it from memory, say it aloud, then drops you into short readings so it lands in context. Underneath, FSRS spaced repetition brings each word back right before you'd forget. No leagues, no mascot guilt.
