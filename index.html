Recalling is learning.

Recall means pulling a word out of your own head before you see the answer, and that effort is what burns it into memory. Vocab Swiper hides the meaning until you've tried, so every card is real practice.

The method

More than flashcards.

Every word travels the same road: recognise it, recall it, then produce it, across all four skills.

Honest recall

Pull each word from memory before you reveal it. Revealing counts as a miss, so your progress can never lie to you.

Spaced repetition

FSRS, the memory model behind modern Anki, brings each word back right before you'd forget it.

Real graded decks

26 languages, thousands of words each, aligned to JLPT / CEFR / HSK and checked against a real dictionary. Never a demo list.

Native voices

Every word and sentence spoken by a native voice, highlighted word-by-word as it reads.

Say it, type it, spell it

As a word sticks, the app asks for more: assemble it from tiles, type it from memory, then say it aloud for pronunciation feedback.

Read it in context

Short, level-matched stories and example sentences, so new words land where they're actually used, not stranded in isolation.

Q&A

Which languages can I learn?
Twenty-six and counting: Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Dutch, Turkish, Polish, Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Greek, Czech, Indonesian, Romanian, Ukrainian, Slovak, Bulgarian, Croatian, Malay and Filipino. Each with a real graded deck of thousands of words aligned to JLPT / CEFR / HSK / TOPIK levels, not a demo list, plus English itself for speakers of the other twenty-five.
What's free, and what's Pro?
Free: every deck, spaced repetition, streaks, quizzes, and an example sentence for each word. Pro: natural human-sounding voices, unlimited AI example sentences and in-context reading, custom topic decks, and a streak shield for the day life gets in the way.
Does it work offline?
Yes. The decks and your reviews live on your phone, so learning works on a plane. AI-generated example sentences and premium voices need a connection; everything else doesn't.
How is this different from other flashcard apps?
Honest recall, and it doesn't stop at cards. The meaning stays hidden until you've tried; revealing counts as a miss, so your stats can't lie to you. As a word sticks, the app makes you produce it: spell it from tiles, type it from memory, say it aloud, then drops you into short readings so it lands in context. Underneath, FSRS spaced repetition brings each word back right before you'd forget. No leagues, no mascot guilt.
