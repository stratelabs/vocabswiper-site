Which one should you use?

Pick Quizlet if
  • you're studying something other than a language
  • your teacher shared a set for class
  • you want games like Match
Pick Vocab Swiper if
  • you're learning a language's vocabulary
  • you want decks that already exist, levelled and in order
  • you'd rather the app decide when to review

Plenty of people use both: Quizlet for class sets, Vocab Swiper for the language itself.

Questions

Is there a free Quizlet alternative for learning languages?

Vocab Swiper is free to download on iPhone and Android, with ready-made vocabulary decks in 56 languages and spaced repetition built in. An optional Pro subscription adds premium voices and some advanced features.

Is Vocab Swiper better than Quizlet?

For learning a language's vocabulary from scratch, it's built for exactly that: graded decks, audio, example sentences and review timing are all done for you. For other subjects, or a set your teacher shared, Quizlet is the better fit.

Can I bring my own word list?

You can add your own words to your own decks, next to the ready-made ones, so a word from class or a show goes straight into your reviews.
